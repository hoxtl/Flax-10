\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

StudyFlax is a time management platform designed to support university students in balancing
the many demands of higher education. It helps students at King's College London coordinate
lectures, deadlines, coursework, society commitments, and personal goals within a single,
unified system. Instead of becoming another task to manage, StudyFlax simplifies organisation
by reducing cognitive load, allowing students to focus their energy on learning and personal
growth. By providing a clear view of both academic and extracurricular responsibilities, the
platform empowers users to plan effectively, make better use of their time, and maintain a
balanced schedule. Teaching staff are also able to use the platform to create and manage
modules, publish reading lists and assignment deadlines, and communicate key course information
directly to enrolled students without relying on external systems.

StudyFlax is built as a Single Page Application accessible on both desktop and mobile.
The frontend is developed with React and Vite, and the backend is a Django REST Framework
application backed by a PostgreSQL database. The development environment is containerised
using Nix Flakes and Docker Compose, providing a reproducible setup across the team.
