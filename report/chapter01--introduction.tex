\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}
StudyFlax is a time management platform built for students in higher education, helping them organise their academic workload, group commitments, and personal goals in one place. Students who struggle to stay on top of a busy and fragmented schedule gain a clearer, more structured view of their time — reducing stress and allowing them to focus on what matters most: their studies.

StudyFlax is built as a Single Page Application, accessible on both desktop and mobile. The frontend is developed with React, supported by a Django and PostgreSQL backend, with the development environment containerised using Nix Flakes.
